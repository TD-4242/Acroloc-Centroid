### Reading the PDF

The Read tool needs a `pages` range (≤20 pages/call). Printed page numbers are **per-chapter**, so PDF-page offsets differ by chapter. Calibrate with the TOC (PDF p.2-4) and by reading a chapter's first page. **Known offsets:** Ch3 printed `3-N` ≈ PDF `N+14`; Ch4 printed `4-N` ≈ PDF `N+67`. Known landmarks: keypad §4.1 = PDF 69-76; parameter-group list (§4.2 intro) = PDF 76; Group 10 table = PDF 101; Group 12 = PDF 106; Group 13 = PDF 111; Group 16 tail / Group 17 = PDF 120-121. Group 09 (Communication) sits just before Group 10 (≈ PDF 95-100). Chapters 5-11 are past the Group tables and the §4.3 per-parameter descriptions (which start at printed 4-64); locate them via the TOC + sampling.
